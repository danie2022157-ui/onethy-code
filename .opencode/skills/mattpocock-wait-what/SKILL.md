# Wait What

## Purpose
Clear up confusion when the user is lost about what happened or why.

## When to use
When the user signals confusion or an unexpected result.

## Core steps
1. State plainly what just happened and in what order.
2. Explain why, referencing concrete evidence or diffs.
3. Clarify any incorrect assumptions.
4. Offer the next sensible step and confirm alignment.
