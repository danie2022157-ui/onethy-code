# Grilling

## Purpose
Use systematic questioning to move a conversation from vague to actionable.

## When to use
During brainstorming or planning when clarity is the bottleneck.

## Core steps
1. Identify the key open question blocking progress.
2. Ask focused questions, one thread at a time.
3. Capture answers and update the shared understanding.
4. Summarize decisions and remaining unknowns.
