# To Questionnaire

## Purpose
Gather the missing inputs needed to proceed by asking structured questions.

## When to use
When a request lacks the information required to act confidently.

## Core steps
1. Identify what must be known before work can start.
2. Group questions logically (goal, constraints, acceptance, edge cases).
3. Keep questions concise and offer defaults where sensible.
4. Use the answers to produce a clear plan or spec.
